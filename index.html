    How to play

    • Drag on the course to aim. Set how far to hit it with − and +, and the green window moves to that distance.
    • Tap once to start the needle, tap again inside the green. Stopping in the green never gives you a horror shot.
    • Wind pushes full shots around. On the green, follow the putt guide and contours. Anything inside 3 ft is a gimme.
    • White stakes are out of bounds: one shot penalty and play again from the same spot.
    • Each level gives 3 skill points. The practice area opens once a day, one go at each challenge.
